Ready in about a minute

  1. Pick a topic

    Type something like “Nordic mythology” or “90s pop”. Choose English or Estonian, the difficulty and the timer.

  2. Invite

    Players go to www.kazoot.app/join and type the code, or scan the QR code on your screen. No app to install.

  3. Play

    Everyone answers on their own device. Correct answers score more the faster they come. Standings after every question.

The organiser doesn’t have to sit out

Usually whoever writes the quiz knows every answer. With Kazoot, questions stay hidden until they appear on screen — for the host too. You start the game from your phone and compete like everyone else.

Teaching or running a work session? Choose “Let me check first” to review and edit the questions before you start. Players see that the host has seen them.

  • Is it OK for schools and children?

    Players only enter a nickname — no account, email or other personal details. Topics are screened and questions are generated for a general audience, but no automated filter is perfect, so teachers should use review mode. Accounts for creating quizzes are for adults.

  • Can the questions be wrong?

    Yes, occasionally. Questions are written by AI and checked automatically for format, duplicates and obvious giveaways, but not by a person. If accuracy matters, for example in class, choose “Let me check first” to review and edit them before playing.

  • Can I show it on a big screen?

    Yes, but you don’t have to: questions and answers appear on every player’s device. If you host from a laptop on a projector, the room can follow along there too.

Questions

Do players need an account or an app?

No. Players open the join page in any browser, type the room code and a name. Only the person creating a quiz on their own topic signs in.

How is the demo different from a real quiz?

The demo uses hand-written quizzes that are the same for everyone, so it costs nothing to run. When you create your own quiz, Kazoot writes fresh questions for your topic. Everything else — joining, timing, scoring — is the same game.

What topics work best?

Broad or well-known subjects: history, geography, science, films, music, sport, food, famous people and places. AI can’t know private things like your colleagues’ habits or a family story, and it declines topics that aren’t suitable for a general audience.

What if someone’s phone disconnects?

They reopen the page and carry on with the same seat and score. If the host drops out, the game waits for them briefly; after that the longest-connected player can keep it moving until the host is back.

How do refunds and cancellation work?

Subscriptions can be cancelled any time from your account and stop at the end of the paid month. If creating a quiz fails, you get the AI game back automatically. See Refunds & cancellation for details.
